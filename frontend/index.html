About WeedVision

WeedVision is an AI-powered smart farming assistant that identifies weeds in crop images and helps farmers make faster, more targeted weed management decisions.

How It Works

1. Capture an Image

Use your camera or upload a clear image of crops and weeds.

2. AI Analysis

3. Review Results

View the processed image, weed information, and recent detection history.
